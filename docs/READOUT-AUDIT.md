# Numeric and graphical readouts in Midnight94 v0.14

Across the 13 devices, 32 of the 52 primary controls use a two-digit `00`–`99` readout. Each rounds the effective parameter to the nearest value on that scale. The endpoints read `00` and `99`. The two control positions immediately on either side of center read `49` and `50`. LFO modulation moves the displayed value while the saved knob position can stay fixed. The table follows the illustrated manual’s order, with synths first and effects second. [Illustrated v0.14 manual](OP-1-Field-Quick-Reference-v0.14.pdf)

Ampig v11’s Drive and Sag numbers show the effective settings. The incoming signal determines how hard the simulated supply is loaded. With Drive, Sag and Mix at `99`, a sustained input can repeatedly interrupt the wet sound. Press Ochre for Recover to change how quickly it returns. On the regular FX page, the Circuit and Recover cards each show a title, a mode name and its icon.
